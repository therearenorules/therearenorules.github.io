4. 보관과 삭제

  • 기기 데이터는 사용자가 앱에서 지우거나 앱을 삭제할 때까지, 익명 계정과 동기화 콘텐츠는 삭제를 요청할 때까지 보관됩니다.
  • iOS 키체인·iCloud 키-값 저장소의 앱 식별자와 Android의 앱 범위 기기 식별자는 운영체제가 관리하므로 앱을 삭제해도 남을 수 있습니다.
  • 푸시 토큰은 앱에서 알림을 끄면 서버에서 비활성화되며, 계정 삭제 요청 시 삭제합니다.
  • 사용 통계 대기열은 설정에서 사용 통계를 끄면 삭제됩니다. 이미 전송된 사용 통계는 삭제 요청 시 또는 처리 목적이 끝나면 삭제합니다.
  • iCloud·Google Drive 백업(덮어쓰기를 선택했을 때 보존되는 직전 백업 1부 포함)과 Android 시스템 백업은 해당 클라우드·기기 백업 설정에서 직접 삭제할 때까지 남을 수 있습니다.
  • 홈페이지 문의는 문의 처리 완료 후 1년이 지나면 삭제합니다.
  • 구매·거래 기록은 구매 복원, 환불, 회계, 분쟁 및 부정 사용 방지를 위해 법률상 필요한 기간 동안 보관될 수 있습니다. 익명 계정과 직접 연결되지 않은 스토어 원거래 기록도 구매 복원과 사기 방지를 위해 남을 수 있습니다.

삭제 시 데이터베이스 행과 인증 계정은 복구가 어렵도록 삭제합니다. 법률상 보존 대상은 접근을 제한한 뒤 보존기간 종료 시 삭제 또는 비식별 처리합니다.

Privacy Policy — English

Last updated and effective: October 11, 2026. Users must be at least 14 in South Korea and meet the locally required minimum age elsewhere. No parental-consent flow is provided.

Information we process

  • Anonymous Supabase user ID, device identifier, OS and version, app version, and device name. Android uses the app-scoped ANDROID_ID; iOS uses an app-generated identifier kept in the Keychain (SecureStore) or iCloud key-value storage, which helps find the same anonymous account after reinstalling.
  • Tarot cards, notes, journals, spreads, timestamps, and settings stored on the device and, if a Premium user turns on Cloud Sync, in Supabase, or, if they turn on iCloud or Google Drive backup, in that cloud.
  • Push token, notification on/off status, and basic device information (device identifier, platform, app version, language) after notification permission is granted.
  • Product, transaction, purchase, expiration, subscription status, and environment. Apple receipts are used only for server verification and are not stored in raw form. Google Play purchase tokens and order numbers are kept with the subscription record to re-check status, apply refunds or cancellations, and restore purchases. Apple or Google handles payment methods.
  • Usage statistics, only if you allow them: app launch (first launch) and session end (duration range), onboarding completion, daily card draws and the time from install to the first draw, memo saves (length range only, never the text), spread start/save (spread type), opening the app from a notification (notification type, hour), plan-screen views, purchase start/result and restore result (product ID, outcome), ad impressions and load failures (placement, error code), and rewarded-ad load/show/reward/close/failure (placement, failure reason, error code), with timestamps, app version/build, and platform, sent to Supabase with the anonymous account ID. From app version 1.4.9, the app asks once when you first use it (after a card draw) and sends nothing until you allow it (off by default). You can change your choice anytime under “Allow usage statistics” in Settings; turning it off deletes events still waiting on the device. App versions before 1.4.9 (released since July 2026) send some of these statistics by default without this setting; email us to delete records already sent.
  • Ads for free users: Google AdMob interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads (unlock a card meaning for 24 hours or try a locked spread once). Where consent is required we follow your Google consent (UMP) choices and, on iOS, your App Tracking Transparency choice; without consent or tracking permission we request non-personalized ads. Where consent is not required, personalized ads may be shown. Google may process consent status, device or advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostics. Premium subscribers do not see ads.
  • Email address, message, and attachments you send to support, and the name or nickname, email address, message, and page language you submit through the website contact form (stored in Supabase).

Purposes and providers

We use the information to provide app features, sync and backup, notifications, purchase validation and restoration, support, security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, usage statistics you allow, and consent-managed advertising (personalized or non-personalized). Providers include Supabase in its South Korea Seoul region, Apple, Google Play, Google AdMob, Expo/APNs/FCM, iCloud, and Google Drive. If Android system backup or device transfer is enabled, the operating system may back up or transfer limited app preferences excluding secure storage. Apple, Google, and Expo data may be processed in the United States and other countries disclosed by those providers over encrypted networks.

Retention, choices, and deletion

On-device data remains until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app; the anonymous account and synced content remain until you request deletion. OS-managed identifiers (iOS Keychain/iCloud key-value storage, Android app-scoped ID) may remain after uninstalling. Push tokens are deactivated when you turn notifications off in the app and deleted on an account deletion request. Pending usage statistics are deleted when you turn the setting off; statistics already sent are deleted on request or when no longer needed. iCloud/Google Drive backups (including the one previous copy kept when you choose to overwrite) and Android system backups remain until you delete them in those settings. Website inquiries are deleted one year after the inquiry is resolved. Transaction records may remain for restoration, refunds, disputes, law, and fraud prevention.

You can allow or stop usage statistics, turn off notifications and backup, and export data in Settings. On iOS, change tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The app does not currently offer a menu to reopen the ad consent form; email us to change your ad consent choice.

Account deletion: on app version 1.4.9 or later, delete it directly in Settings → Data Management → “Delete Account & Data.” On earlier versions, or if you have already removed the app, email changsekwon@gmail.com with the subject “Tarot Timer account deletion request.” See Delete account and data for the steps and scope. This does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. A new anonymous account may be created if you use the app again.

For access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other privacy requests, email changsekwon@gmail.com.
